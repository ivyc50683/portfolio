Ivy Chan

6+ years conducting mixed-methods human research across startups, enterprise, and academic settings, translating human behavioural insights into product strategy and AI development.

Background

I studied Psychology and Commerce at the University of Auckland, graduating in 2017 with first-class honours — it was there I became genuinely obsessed with how people think and make decisions. I found my way into tech through recruitment in 2018, connecting engineers and designers with startups, which quickly pulled me into the industry. That led me to PALO IT, a tech consultancy, where managing people and culture gave me a real feel for how great tech teams are built and what makes them tick.

By 2019, people started noticing my psychology background, and I moved into UX Research and Agile Consulting — running mixed-methods research for clients like LVMH, BNP, and QBE on how people navigate complex digital systems. What I loved most was seeing that work actually shape product roadmaps and algorithmic design decisions, not just sit in a report.

In 2021 I joined an Alibaba-backed ed-tech startup as their first technical hire, doing everything — research, product, design, investor pitches. Then in 2022, I joined Maphive, an Apple indoor map data supplier, leading product across apps for visually impaired users, property management platforms, and mapping tools with a team of 11. We shipped something new every single week.

Then I made a deliberate pivot back into research. I completed my MSc at UCL in 2024 with Distinction, on the Dean's List, writing a thesis on how the brain makes sense of continuous experience — now preparing for publication. I also won the UCL AI Foundry that year, building a speech recognition system for hearing-impaired users. Now I'm a Research Assistant at UCL's Institute of Cognitive Neuroscience, with a PhD on the way.
